Design system, version 3

Paper from a clinic patient card, blue-black from chest X-ray film, the red of World TB Day for TB itself, teal for care. Everything else stays quiet so the route line and the dot field can carry the site.

Version 3: red means TB, teal means care

The sun yellow, butter, mint and deep-teal panels from version 2 are gone. The brief never had them, and together they made the site look generic. The red of World TB Day replaces coral: it marks page titles, the wordmark, "you are here", urgent states and illness data, and it never goes on a button. Teal carries the route and every action. Paper and ink do the rest.

Glass on the bars only

The sticky header and the phone tab bar use a frosted material: Paper at 72% with a 24px blur, so the page shows through as you scroll. Content, rows and cards never use glass. When the browser can't blur, or Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast is on, both bars turn solid Paper.

Route line and stops

A 6px teal line on a Rule track. When results load it draws in stop by stop and each stop fills in. That is the planner's only motion, and it is skipped when reduced motion is on.

The dot unit

One dot is 1,000 people who fell ill with TB in 2024. The counts are computed from headline_2024, divided by 1,000 and rounded: 724 fell ill, 179 never found, 37 died. Every state changes shape as well as color.

Fell ill

Filled, Dot. All 724 at the start.

Never found

Hollow red ring, stroke about a third of the radius. 179.

Died

Solid Ink. 37, chosen from dots that were found, so each count stays exact.

Size

The field fits its stage: about 8px dots on an 11px pitch on a phone, up to 24px on a 34px pitch on desktop. On the Data page an 8px dot returns as a unit marker.

Charts

Plain-language title, one-line takeaway, direct labels, then source and date. Horizontal gridlines in Rule, a baseline in Ink. Context in Dot; the subject in TB red (illness) or Teal (care). Ranges are bands or whiskers with a caption. On phones, time series become one row per year.

Layout and motion

  • One left-aligned reading column, 34em. Charts break out to 1,040px; the dot field runs to the edge of the screen.
  • Gutter 20–64px. Spacing steps: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 72, 112.
  • Whitespace and a single rule separate things. The only box on the site is the screening summary, drawn as a clinic patient card.
  • Two motion moments: the route line drawing in, and the dot field on the Data page. With reduced motion, both show their end state at once.

What changed in version 3, and the generic-look check

  • Version 2 used about 20 tints on Home. Version 3 uses eight colors: Paper, Field, Ink, Muted, Rule, Teal, Teal deep and TB red, plus Dot and Film for the data.
  • TB red replaces Coral and Coral deep. It passes AA as text, so one red covers titles, the 911 link and the URGENT screen. On Film it lightens to #F0646C.
  • Chips and badges are squared to 4px. The pills and the row of check icons under the planner are gone; the reassurance lines are plain text between rules.
  • One solid teal button per screen. The action repeated on every row is outlined, and "Call before you go" is a text link.
  • Glass appears on two surfaces, both navigation. The source of truth lists glass cards as a generic-look sign, and Apple's guidance keeps glass off content.
  • Muted stays #56626A; the original #5E6A70 dropped to 4.4:1 on Paper 2.
  • The 724 dots start in Dot, a blue-grey from the Ink family. Red appears only as the 179 hollow rings, and the 37 deaths can turn solid Ink.
  • Checked: no eyebrows, single-word highlights, decorative numbering, shadowed cards, gradients, scroll fades, monospace labels, arrow buttons, middle-dot strings or hairline columns.
