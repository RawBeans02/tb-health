Color directions

The same Home screen on a phone, in three palettes. All three remove the sun yellow, butter, mint and deep-teal blocks, the row of check icons and the pill shapes. My pick is 1b.

1a

Spec palette, strict

Paper, ink and one teal for the route and every button. Coral marks only "you are here" and urgent. This is what the source of truth asks for.

Trade-off: calm, but nothing says TB at a glance.

TB red for TB, teal for care

World TB Day red replaces coral: page titles, the wordmark, "you are here", urgent and the illness data. Teal still carries the route and every button.

Trade-off: two strong colors, so red never goes on a button.

Red route, ink for the rest

World TB Day red carries the route line, the stops and the main button. Everything else is ink. The most recognisable as TB.

Trade-off: a red main button can read as danger, so the urgent screen switches to black.
